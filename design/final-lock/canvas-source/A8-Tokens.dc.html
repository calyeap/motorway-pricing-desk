Semantic colour rules
Red — brand, the active step, the one primary button on a screen, the subject rule, the decision block rule. Never on data.
Amber — look here: review deltas, needs‑attention rows, unticked‑by‑default reasons, the Advisory label. Never means “bad”.
Sage — confirmed: included tick, completed step, parsed‑OK count, Confirm action. Never means “good deal”.
Grey — normal deltas, secondary fields, excluded / set‑aside rows, Not stated.
Charcoal — deterministic: subject stage and Maximum acquisition price. Nothing else is filled charcoal.
No green/red for price judgement. No gradients, no glow, no shadows. Excluded = 60 % text tone, never opacity on inputs.
