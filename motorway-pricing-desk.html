Step 1 of 3 · Comparables

Reading SGCarMart results…

Fields the page does not state are left as Not stated. Nothing is filled in.

Comparable listings

  • Small lines under each figure are the plain difference from the car being appraised (mileage, owners, COE months) or from the median of the ticked comparables (depreciation).
  • Higher / lower depreciation vs selected comps: the listing's yearly depreciation is 10% or more above or below the median depreciation of the ticked comparables. It is a cue to look at why. It does not say the asking price is wrong.
  • Renewed COE: the COE has been renewed, so the asking price and depreciation are not like-for-like with an original-COE car. Short COE: under 2 years left on the original COE. Registered 9+ yrs ago: the car is near or past its 10-year point.
  • Amber figures cross a review threshold: depreciation ±10% vs the selected median, mileage 20,000 km or more from this car, a different owner count, or a renewed / short COE.
  • Not stated: the listing did not give that detail. Nothing is filled in for it. Sold listings (SGCarMart's own sold status) have no asking price and never count in the figures.
